IamHome Agent: version history
==============================
What each version brought, newest first. Requires umbrelOS 2.0 or newer.
(Generated from CHANGELOG.md with node tools/changelog-txt.mjs.)

2.3.0 (13 Nov 2026)
-------------------
- Knowledgebase tab at the top next to Overview: the whole knowledge base for
  reading, also in the free version. I want… guides, When something doesn't
  work, articles on the apps you have installed and 25 articles from the
  Intellihome wiki (Home Assistant, Node-RED, Homebridge, Docker, MQTT,
  Zigbee, Thread, Matter, UniFi…, the same as intellihome.cz/znalosti), with
  search, in Czech and English. With a licence Walk me through it with the
  agent / Ask the agent. The agent knows the wiki too and links to articles in
  its answers.
- How to: problems on Home, morning report findings (in the e-mail and in
  Overview → Reports) and recommendations link to the matching Knowledgebase
  article (full disk, hot server, app not running, backups, log errors, Home
  Assistant).

2.2.9 (13 Nov 2026)
-------------------
- Security library updates: hono 4.13.18 and @hono/node-server 2.1.8 (pulled
  in by the MCP SDK), fixing vulnerabilities reported on 13 Nov.

2.2.8 (13 Nov 2026)
-------------------
- Known errors. Next to a log error (Overview → Logs, app card, Home
  Assistant) there is a mark as known button, optionally with a "valid until"
  date (irreversible-action password). Such an error, e.g. a switched-off
  heater that Node-RED keeps looking for, stops counting towards the morning
  report and error increases. It stays watched: if there are 10× more of it
  than usual, the report flags it. New errors are still reported. List and
  removal at the bottom of Overview → Logs. The chat agent treats them as
  known and doesn't present them as a problem.
- "I know" also for Home Assistant devices and apps. A device that is down on
  purpose (heating in summer), once marked on the Home Assistant card, doesn't
  count as not working, on Home or in the report; once it works again, the
  report mentions it once. An app you only start occasionally can be marked
  right in Overview → Apps (previously only in Settings); its stopping is then
  not reported.
- The end of an "I know" period is announced by the morning report 3 days
  before and the day after.
- Suggestions: an error that repeats for 14 days in a similar number while the
  app isn't crashing is offered by the app ("Do you know about it?") on Home,
  in Overview → Logs and once in the morning report. Likewise a Home Assistant
  device that has been down for over a week.
- The chat agent can save "I know" itself when you tell it ("treat these
  errors as known until March"); you confirm with the password.
- Ignored errors (Settings → Monitoring) are shown in Overview → Logs with
  what they hid in 7 days, and one button converts them to known errors that
  are watched for an increase.

2.2.7 (13 Nov 2026)
-------------------
- Up-to-date backup monitoring. The backup folder is checked at every check
  (every 15 minutes) instead of once every 6 hours, so the agent and Home
  could show a backup several hours old (with the hourly incremental umbrelOS
  backup the agent said "last one at 10:17" at 3 pm). Large folders (the
  umbrelOS backup repository has thousands of files) are walked starting with
  the most recently changed subfolders, so the newest backup is found in them
  too. Home and the instant answer say when the folder was checked.
- The agent gives the check time for monitoring data ("as of the check at
  14:30") and does not present it as the current state. When the data is old
  it verifies it live (for backups it walks the folder itself).

2.2.6 (12 Nov 2026)
-------------------
- App restarts and updates. Restarts, starts, stops and updates of apps are
  read from the Umbrel log (even when they took only a few seconds, which the
  15-minute check cannot see), together with who did them: an IamHome task,
  the agent in chat, manually in Umbrel or an automatic update. They are on
  the app card, the ones outside IamHome also in Outages and in "What happened
  overnight?". "No outage since" starts at the last restart. After the update
  they are also filled in from the stored logs of the last 7 days.
- The agent answers "did the app restart…?" from these events (it used to say
  "no" when an IamHome task restarted the app between two checks). Before
  answering "it didn't happen / I found nothing" it says what it checked. When
  it reads an app's log it gets the app's known messages from the knowledge
  base (so far Homebridge: the avahi messages "Server startup complete …
  umbrel-N.local" are not restarts).
- New instant answer "Did the scheduled tasks run?" (no AI, free): for every
  enabled task, its runs due in the last 24 hours and whether they ran, failed
  or did not run at all (for a missed run also that IamHome was not running at
  that time).

2.2.5 (10 Nov 2026)
-------------------
- Home → Apps: the running status is on a line below the app name, long names

2.2.4 (9 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Server restart without false alarms. For the first 15 minutes after the
  server starts, apps that are still starting up are not reported as down
  (previously every restart sent "⚠️ problem" and a quarter of an hour later
  "✅ recovered"). The Overview shows them as "starting after the server
  start", Home shows a notice. If an app isn't up after 15 minutes, you get an
  alert.
- Home Assistant after a restart. After a restart HA sets the last change of
  every entity to the start time, so devices down for months looked like a new
  fault. The app now remembers since when a device has been down; for a device
  it first sees after an HA restart it says "HA start, maybe longer".
- Morning report: a camera in "motion only" mode is no longer reported as "not
  recording". A crashed app and its web count as one thing (previously two
  crashed apps looked like a "simultaneous outage of 4 services").
- Verification after an update and after a task action checks that the app's
  container really runs (Umbrel reports "ready" even for a crashed container).
- The monthly report card tells apart when the server was down (from a
  detected restart) and when only monitoring was (app update, Umbrel MCP
  outage). Backup days are counted in local time.
- Overview charts break the line where there is no data (the server or
  monitoring wasn't running). Quick answers warn when the last measurement is
  old and say when the internet speed test ran. The memory leak forecast
  starts only after the app's or server's last restart.

2.2.3 (9 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Crashed container. Umbrel shows an app as running even when its container
  has crashed. The app now detects it (the app uses no memory) and reports it
  as a "container down" outage: e-mail, Home, morning report and automatic
  restart.
- Server restart. A umbrelOS restart is recorded in Outages, in the quick
  answers (night, week) and in the morning report. Apps now show "no outage
  for X", at most since the server started, instead of a misleading "running ·
  20 days".
- Server uptime in the Overview is counted from its start (was the value from
  the last check), under an hour in minutes.

2.2.2 (9 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Forgotten irreversible-action password. Every field asking for this password
  (unlocking Settings, confirming an action, tasks, updates) has a "Forgot
  password?" link. A second click logs you out and opens password recovery
  with the irreversible-action password change preselected (code from the app
  log).
- Clearer morning report. The e-mail has a coloured header by status, disk /
  RAM / CPU / temperature tiles with a bar, findings as coloured cards, the AI
  analysis with headings and bullets and the server status as a table. The
  plain-text version stays.

2.2.1 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Stricter lockout against guessing. After 10 failed attempts from one address
  within an hour (login password, API token, wizard code, pairing, forgotten
  password) the address is blocked for an hour (was 20 attempts and 10
  minutes).
- Same for the irreversible-action and backup passwords: after 10 wrong action
  passwords, or 5 wrong backup passwords, within an hour a one-hour block (was
  10 minutes).

2.2.0 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Secrets are not stored in history either. Tokens and passwords from the
  settings and recognisable keys (Claude, OpenAI, GitHub, HA tokens) are
  masked in saved conversations (including the title), the action history,
  notes and e-mail previews. This also applies to a token you paste into a
  message to the agent.

2.1.9 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- HTTPS keys encrypted. The private keys of the app's own certificate
  authority and server (data/tls) are encrypted with the same key. On another
  device the app creates a new certificate authority.
- Secret settings encrypted. Tokens and passwords of other services (MCP, AI
  key, e-mail, Home Assistant, Node-RED, UniFi…) are encrypted in config.json
  with a key from Umbrel (APP_SEED), so nobody with access only to the data
  folder (e.g. another AI agent via Umbrel MCP) can read them. Backups still
  carry them and restoring works on another device too.
- App passwords are no longer stored readably. The login password and the
  irreversible-action password are kept in the settings (config.json) only as
  a hash (scrypt). On the first start of the new version they are converted
  automatically; devices are logged out once.
- Stronger backup password. A new backup needs a password of at least 12
  characters and 3 of 4 types (uppercase, lowercase, digits, special
  characters); the field has a generate button and a reminder to save the
  password. Older backups can still be restored.

2.1.8 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- One MCP token in Settings. The field is now just “Umbrel MCP token”
  (monitoring and chat). A separate chat token is optional and hidden under “+
  Separate token for chat (optional)”, because Umbrel currently gives all
  tokens the same permissions. If it is already filled in, it stays visible.

2.1.7 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Wizard: Home / Admin choice now says explicitly that you can switch to the
  extended Admin view after finishing, in Settings → Server → Mode (and back).
- Stronger irreversible-action password. A new password must have at least 10
  characters and at least 2 of 4 types (uppercase, lowercase, digits, special
  characters). The current password keeps working.
- AI tasks need the password. Saving or enabling any scheduled AI task (in the
  form or via the chat agent) needs the irreversible-action password and sends
  a 🔐 e-mail, like tasks that change apps.
- Daily spend limit for AI tasks (Settings → Web UI and agent, default $1).
  Once used up, AI tasks don't run until midnight and one e-mail is sent.

2.1.6 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Stronger login password. A new login password (wizard, Settings, forgotten
  password) must have, besides 20 characters, at least 3 of 4 types:
  uppercase, lowercase, digits, special characters. The generated password
  contains all of them. The current password keeps working.
- Model picker shows only configured providers. Chat and the task form offer
  only Claude (with an API key), Ollama (with an address or model filled in)
  and the OpenAI compatible models set in Settings → AI model.

2.1.5 (8 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Help: spend limit directly with the AI provider. For the API key (Anthropic
  and OpenAI) the help recommends also setting a monthly limit in the console
  where the key is created: if the key leaks, only this limit stops the
  spending.
- Free version without chat. Without a licence (and after the trial) the agent
  chat, conversations, notes, model picker and spend are hidden (a question
  ended with an error anyway). Quick answers without AI are on Home.
- 7 new quick answers without AI: What happened last night?, What is loading
  the server now?, Is the temperature OK?, Which apps have errors?, What did
  the morning report say?, What are the tasks doing?, About the server.
- Logout after 10 minutes of inactivity (was 5).
- Overview sections in the side menu instead of tabs at the top, grouped into
  Server / Apps & network / History & admin. In Chat the menu still shows
  conversations. On a phone, tapping the section name in the top bar also
  opens the menu.
- Security: a question from an “Ask” or “Why? / What to do?” button
  (containing log text, device names or e-mails) is treated by the agent as
  data, not instructions, and the action confirmation card warns about it.
- Free version without locked tabs: the Home Assistant, app, UniFi, API and
  Spending tabs are hidden; the menu has “What a licence adds” instead.

2.1.1 (5 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Annual licence ending notice: in the last 7 days a window after login (Renew
  licence / Remind me tomorrow) and a notice in the side menu, Home from 30
  days ahead, e-mail 14 and 3 days before and on the last day, a line in the
  morning report. The window also warns before the trial ends (last 3 days).
- 7-day grace period after an annual licence ends: paid features keep running
  for another week with a notice (time for the payment and the key), then the
  free version runs.
- Remove licence: in Settings → Licence (with the password for irreversible
  actions) the key can be deleted from the installation; the trial then runs
  if it has not ended yet, otherwise the free version.
- Revoking a wrongly issued key: the issuer can revoke a key; the app learns
  it from the daily check-in and stops accepting it (trial or free version,
  Settings → Licence says why).
- Renewal without losing days: a new annual licence continues from the end of
  the current one. The Renew licence button opens the request with the annual
  licence preselected.

2.0.5 (3 Nov 2026)
------------------
- OpenAI in chat: OpenAI reasoning models (gpt-6-luna, gpt-6.1-terra…)
  rejected the agent's tools ("Function tools with reasoning_effort are not
  supported"). The app now retries with reasoning turned off and remembers it
  for the model. Models where reasoning cannot be turned off (gpt-6.1-sol)
  automatically switch to the OpenAI Responses API. Verified: gpt-6-luna and
  gpt-6.1-sol; Settings notes to contact support for other models.
- OpenAI model reasoning can be set in Settings → AI model (low, medium, high)
  for models that cannot turn it off.
- Fixes from a code review: a task with an OpenAI model can only be saved with
  OpenAI set up, a clear error for a damaged backup, OPENAI_REASONING in the
  .env export for the terminal agent.

2.0.4 (3 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Backup and restore: Settings → Backup and restore downloads an encrypted

  optionally with history and conversations. It is protected by its own backup
  password. Restore from one file in Settings or right in the wizard on a new
  installation.
- The first-run wizard offers ChatGPT (OpenAI) next to Claude in the AI step,
  with a model choice.
- The missing AI key warning also disappears when OpenAI is set up.

2.0.3 (3 Nov 2026)

2.0.0 (2 Nov 2026)
------------------
- Home / Admin mode: the wizard asks "I'm installing for my family / I'm a
  pro". Home has a simple view and an agent that talks without technical terms
  (numbers in brackets), Admin has everything as before. Switch in Settings →
  Server.
- Home view: a traffic light and one sentence about the server, what needs
  doing (with advice and an Ask button), what's fine, recommendations (backup,
  e-mail, remote access…) and the monthly report card.
- Instant answers, no AI, free: in an empty chat "Is everything OK?", "What
  happened this week?", "What takes up space?", "Is the backup OK?", "Which
  batteries need replacing?" and more; "Why? / What to do?" hands the answer
  to the agent.
- I want…: tested guides (phone photos, movies at home, remote access, ad
  blocking, smart home, files, passwords, backup) and fixes for common
  problems in a knowledge base; the agent guides you step by step.
- Home Assistant by room: devices that aren't working (not entities) with
  advice on what to try, and batteries by room.
- Backups and space: watching the backup folder (NAS, drive), a notice when
  the Umbrel backup didn't run, the size of each app and a forecast of its
  growth.
- Safe updates: release notes, "Is it safe?" and "Update and verify" (with the
  irreversible-action password; afterwards it checks that the new version
  runs, the web responds and the log has no errors).
- Monthly report card: on the 1st of each month an e-mail summing up the month
  (availability, outages, disk, backup, updates) with a grade.
- Also: detailed logs for the agent store placeholders instead of e-mails and
  public IP addresses, a UniFi camera in motion-only mode no longer reports a
  false "not recording", clearer headings in Settings.
